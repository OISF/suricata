What is Suricata
================

Suricata is a high-performance, open-source network threat detection engine providing real-time intrusion detection (IDS), inline intrusion prevention (IPS), network security monitoring (NSM), and offline pcap processing in a single engine. It is trusted by security teams, governments, researchers, and security product vendors around the world.

Suricata is developed, maintained, and supported by the `Open Information Security Foundation (OISF) <https://oisf.net/>`_ together with a global community of contributors and consortium members.

About the Open Information Security Foundation (OISF)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The Open Information Security Foundation (OISF) is a 501(c)(3) non-profit foundation dedicated to building and sustaining Suricata. Since
2009, OISF has provided the long-term stewardship an open-source project of Suricata's scale requires: a professional, globally distributed development team, board governance, and the business infrastructure that keeps the project stable, secure, and moving forward.

OISF is vendor-neutral. Its work is guided by the needs of the entire Suricata community, not any single company or product. The foundation is funded by its consortium members, organizations that rely on Suricata and invest in its continued development, and it has no outside investors.

Beyond the codebase, OISF brings the community together through SuriCon, its annual user and developer conference, along with training and community
programs that help users and contributors get the most out of Suricata.
